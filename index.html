LOOK OUT FOR YOURSELF

Stay safe.

A few good habits make online spaces better for everyone.

Protect personal information

Keep your home address, school, phone number, passwords and live location private.

Be thoughtful with photos

Only share content you are comfortable with others seeing. Never send intimate images.

Pause before meeting anyone

Do not arrange private meetups with people you only know online. Talk with a trusted adult.

Block, report and get help

If someone pressures, threatens or makes you uncomfortable, stop responding and seek help from a trusted adult.

Keep it respectful

No bullying, harassment, hate, sexual content or pressure. Treat people kindly.
